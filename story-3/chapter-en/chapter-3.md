forty if you counted my previous life, but we would leave that aside for

Arus should've spoken up instead of letting Aisha cover for them.
They both should have pushed back against me. He should've thought with

no way I could accept their relationship when he couldn't do that much.

Seeing him like this made me wonder if Aisha had been cleaning up
after him every time he messed up. Let's say I told them, If you both love

She'd been practically attached to Arus since he'd been born. I'd left

about herself, she had no way of educating him. She had to do some

Considering her past actions, she'd probably learn and grow from
this. I highly doubted that Arus would.
Because he didn't say or do anything earlier. Yeah, this isn't gonna
work. I need to separate them for now.
That's why I was against this.

"No." Where was that even coming from? It was true that Ariel had

Had I been wrong?

I'd thought I couldn't convince her, but it seemed I'd gotten through
