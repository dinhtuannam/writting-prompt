# Instructions for AI Agents

Before changing or creating story content in this repository:

1. Read the root `README.md` for repository purpose, publishing boundaries, and the author's delegation of creative authority.
2. For a specific novel, read its `story-bible-index.md` first. Follow its source-of-truth and retrieval workflow before drafting or updating canon.
3. For a new novel, use the requested seed in `story-seeds/` (created from `story-template/novel-seed.md`), copy the full `story-template/` into a new root folder named `story-<id>`, preserve a copy of the seed in the story, and initialize the story bible there. Check that the destination does not already exist. Never build the novel inside the reusable template.
4. Draft chapters only in `<story>/manuscript/chapters/`. The root-level `<story>/chapters/` is public website output and must only contain approved public chapters.
5. The author delegates ordinary creative decisions to the AI. Develop the story, world, characters, plot, chapter events, and ending autonomously within the seed and hard constraints. Do not ask for routine approval.
6. Ask the author only for a high-impact creative fork with materially different strong outcomes, a conflict with an explicit hard constraint, or a genuine block after checking the bible and trying viable directions. When asking, give concise options and an AI recommendation; otherwise keep writing.
7. Maintain the story bible as working source material: index new entities with stable IDs, update canon/plan/status snapshots after creative decisions and completed chapters, and record conflicts/retcons. Do not silently contradict established facts.
8. Before finishing a story task, verify related IDs, paths, plan alignment, knowledge state, timeline, open threads, and whether summaries/indexes need updates.

The story-specific `lore/02-story-rules.md` and explicit author instructions define content and style constraints. They do not revoke the creative delegation above unless the author explicitly says so.
